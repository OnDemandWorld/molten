# Molten 2.0 — App Store materials (PRD §11)

## What's New (v2.0)

Molten 2.0 is now a free download, and your documents join the conversation.

**Knowledge (Plus):** turn your folders into a private knowledge base. Drop
PDFs, text, Markdown, RTF, HTML, DOCX or images into the shared Molten folder
in iCloud Drive; your Mac does the heavy processing once, and cited answers
follow you to iPhone and iPad. Scanned PDFs get OCR. Everything stays on your
devices and your own iCloud — no Molten servers, no account.

**Free for everyone:** conversation search, export (Markdown/JSON/zip) and
import, auto-generated titles, and a context-usage meter.

**Thank you, Early Supporters.** Everyone who bought Molten before 2.0 gets
Molten Plus forever: every Plus feature we ship, at no cost. Pro will be a
separate, optional upgrade.

## Pricing (set in App Store Connect)

- Plus: US$2.99/month, US$19.99/year (1-week free trial), US$49.99 lifetime.
- Pro: NOT created in 2.0 (reserved level 1).

## App Review note (§11.3)

Molten moves from paid-upfront to freemium. The price will be set to Free
before release. All 1.x buyers are automatically granted Plus forever via
AppTransaction detection (no account, no restore needed). The paywall appears
only when a Free limit is reached: Knowledge ▸ add an 11th document (or create
a second base / enable iCloud). A lifetime purchase is available alongside
the subscriptions.
