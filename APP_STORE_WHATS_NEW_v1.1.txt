What's New in Molten 1.1

Accessibility & Polish
• VoiceOver labels and improved hit targets throughout
• Drag-to-dismiss keyboard on the message list (iOS)
• Respects Reduce Motion accessibility setting
• Dismissible error banner for connection issues
• Clearer analytics labels ("input speed" vs "prompt eval rate")

Settings & Connection

Stability

• Fixed text-to-speech silently failing on iOS
• Fixed hotkeys triggering system actions alongside custom shortcuts
• Privacy hardening and analytics accuracy improvements
• Numerous concurrency fixes for reliability

Privacy: Molten collects no data. Ever.
