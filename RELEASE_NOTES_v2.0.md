# What's New in Molten 2.0

**Molten 2.0 is a free download — and your documents join the conversation.**

## Knowledge (Plus)

Turn your folders into a private knowledge base.

- Drop PDFs, text, Markdown, RTF, HTML, DOCX or images into the shared
  **Molten** folder in iCloud Drive — one folder per base.
- Your Mac does the heavy processing **once**: extraction, OCR for scanned
  PDFs and images, and on-device embeddings (MLX, Apple Silicon). iPhone and
  iPad reuse the finished index — nothing is reprocessed when you rename,
  move, or duplicate a file, or open it on another device.
- Ask questions in any chat with a base attached (or type `@Base name …`)
  and get **cited answers** — tap a citation chip to open the source.
- Hybrid search: semantic + keyword, merged for relevance. Keyword search
  works before the embedding model downloads, and offline.
- Smart processing: iPhone defers to your Mac when it's around, or indexes
  while charging; background processing survives app switches; per-file
  status with retry.
- Everything stays on your devices and your own iCloud. No Molten servers,
  no account. For end-to-end encryption, turn on Advanced Data Protection.

**Free tier:** 1 base, up to 10 documents, on this device. "Use a document
once in this chat" is free for everyone.

## Conversations & Context (Free for everyone)

- **Search** across all your conversations, jump straight to the match.
- **Export** any conversation as Markdown or JSON (with optional analytics),
  or everything as a zip. **Import** from JSON.
- **Auto-titles** generated by Apple's on-device model — editable, and can
  be turned off.
- **Context meter** in the composer: estimated usage vs your model's context
  window, with warnings before you hit the limit. History is budgeted
  automatically so long chats keep working.

## Under the Hood

- New module architecture: Entitlements, Knowledge (container/watch/
  process/embed/store/retrieve/UI), Context, Conversations, Share Extension.
- Content-addressed index files (`.mkt`/`.mkv`), SQLite FTS5 + vector
  search, reciprocal rank fusion, per-stage processing claims with leases,
  device heartbeats, coordinated iCloud I/O.
- "Add to Molten Knowledge" share extension on iOS.
- Versioned SwiftData migration; 133 automated tests (XCTest + Swift
  Testing), including on-device embedding verification (cross-device
  cosine > 0.999) and performance acceptance (100-page PDF < 60s;
  20,000-chunk search < 200ms).
- Two thorough code-review rounds: 22 bugs found and fixed before release.
